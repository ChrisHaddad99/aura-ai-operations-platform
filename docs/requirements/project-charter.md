# AURA — AI Unified Response & Automation

**Document:** Project Charter  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Initial baseline

## 1. Project Vision

AURA is a reusable, AI-powered business operations automation platform designed to help small and medium-sized businesses automate repetitive operational processes through intelligent request processing, AI-assisted decision-making, workflow orchestration and external system integrations.

## 2. Business Problem

Small and medium-sized businesses frequently rely on manual processes to manage:

- Customer communications.
- Service requests.
- Administrative activities.
- Information retrieval.
- Task creation and assignment.
- Customer follow-ups.
- Internal coordination.

These processes consume employee time, introduce operational inefficiencies and increase the likelihood of human error.

## 3. Proposed Solution

AURA will provide an intelligent automation platform capable of:

- Receiving information from external sources.
- Understanding and classifying incoming requests.
- Extracting structured information.
- Retrieving relevant business knowledge.
- Making context-aware decisions.
- Executing approved business actions.
- Requesting human approval when necessary.
- Tracking operational processes.
- Recording execution history and outcomes.

## 4. Target Customers

Initial target:
- Small businesses.
- Technical service providers.
- Maintenance companies.
- Engineering consultancies.

Future target:
- Medium-sized enterprises.
- Industrial businesses.
- Professional service companies.
- Technology companies.

## 5. Core Design Principles

1. Modular architecture.
2. Provider-independent AI integration where practical.
3. Human oversight for sensitive decisions.
4. Security by design.
5. Observable AI execution.
6. Reliable workflow execution.
7. Configurable business integrations.
8. Portable deployment architecture.

## 6. Initial Demonstration Scenario

A technical maintenance company receives customer requests through email and web forms.

AURA processes these requests, identifies customers, determines request categories and priorities, retrieves relevant technical information, creates operational tasks and coordinates the required follow-up.

Low-risk activities may be executed automatically.

High-risk activities require explicit human authorization.

## 7. Project Success

The project will be considered successful when it demonstrates:

- End-to-end automated request processing.
- Reliable structured information extraction.
- Contextual knowledge retrieval.
- AI-assisted decision-making.
- External system integration.
- Human approval workflows.
- Persistent operational state.
- Failure handling and recovery.
- AI observability and evaluation.
- Automated testing and deployment.
- Measurable business value.
